# Community Code of Conduct

This policy adapts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Everyone should be able to participate safely, across backgrounds, identities,
abilities and levels of experience. Treat people with empathy, welcome differing
perspectives and accept feedback. Debate technical evidence without attacking
people. Take responsibility when your actions cause harm.

Harassment, threats, unwanted sexual attention, discriminatory insults,
publication of private information and retaliation against reporters are
unacceptable. These standards apply in project spaces and when representing
the project elsewhere.

Maintainers review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy and may remove
content or restrict participation. Depending on severity and repeated behavior,
responses range from a private correction or warning to temporary or permanent
exclusion. Immediate restrictions may be necessary for threats or privacy abuse.
Maintainers should explain decisions and allow a response where safe.

Use a private contact route listed on [the maintainer's profile](https://github.com/nvtank).
If none is available, request a confidential reporting channel in a minimal
issue without naming people or disclosing incident details. A dedicated private
conduct channel remains a follow-up before broader community launch.
